# Safety Validation

Safety validation is mandatory before powered multi-axis operation.

## Required controls

- Physical emergency stop that removes or inhibits actuator energy through the validated safety architecture.
- Independent mechanical hard stops where required by the mechanism.
- Software travel limits with conservative provisional values during commissioning.
- Over-temperature and over-current fault handling.
- Communications timeout handling.
- Fault-latched safe state until an intentional reset procedure is completed.
- Guarding for pinch, crush, shear, and entanglement hazards.
- Controlled test envelope with no personnel inside the active motion zone during initial tests.

## Validation stages

1. Visual and dimensional inspection.
2. Electrical continuity and polarity inspection.
3. E-stop continuity and safe-state verification.
4. Sensor-only test.
5. Single-axis unloaded fixture test.
6. Multi-axis low-speed unloaded test.
7. Limit-switch and software-limit verification.
8. Thermal and current logging.
9. Incremental payload testing using a controlled fixture.
10. Repeated duty-cycle and fault-injection testing.

Final risk assessment, safeguarding, actuator sizing, structural analysis, and applicable regulatory compliance must be completed by qualified personnel for the actual machine.
