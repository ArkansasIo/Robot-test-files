# ARM12 Engineering Plan

## Mechanical baseline
- ARM01 is the master module.
- ARM02–ARM12 inherit the same mounting, joint, cable, controller, and wrist interfaces.
- Conceptual link lengths: 400 mm upper link + 400 mm forearm + up to 400 mm telescoping extension.
- Twelve arm bases are referenced at 30° increments around a central chassis.

## Subsystems
1. Base mounting and bearing structure
2. Shoulder, elbow, and wrist actuation
3. Telescoping forearm and extension drive
4. Modular tool flange
5. Cable carrier and strain relief
6. Local MCU and field bus
7. Power distribution and E-stop
8. Sensor/telemetry system
9. High-level task/vision controller

## Validation sequence
1. Dimensional inspection
2. Electrical continuity and polarity
3. Sensor-only bring-up
4. Single-axis fixture testing
5. Multi-axis low-speed testing
6. Limit and E-stop validation
7. Thermal/current logging
8. Incremental payload testing
9. Duty-cycle testing

Do not treat conceptual dimensions or component placeholders as production-certified engineering values.
